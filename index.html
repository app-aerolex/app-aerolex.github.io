AeroLex

An aviation dictionary and study app for Android. Abbreviations, contractions and aviation words from FAA, ICAO and EASA sources, with quizzes and games to help them stick.

Support

Questions, bug reports, a term that's wrong or missing: email aerolex.app@gmail.com. From inside the app, Settings has Send feedback, and every entry has Report a problem.

If you bought Pro and it isn't showing on a new phone, open Settings and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Google account.
